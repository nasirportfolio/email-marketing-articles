HTML Email Signature vs Plain Text Signature: Which Is Better?

Published: August 2026

Author: Muhammad Nasir Uddin

Email signatures are an important part of professional communication. Businesses and professionals can use either a simple plain text signature or a branded HTML email signature. But which option is better?

What Is a Plain Text Email Signature?

A plain text email signature contains basic information such as your name, job title, phone number, email address, and website. It does not use advanced formatting, images, buttons, or graphic elements.

A simple example might include:

  • Name and job title
  • Company name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Website address

What Is an HTML Email Signature?

An HTML email signature is a professionally designed signature created with HTML and CSS. It can include branding, logos, profile photos, clickable links, social media icons, and call-to-action buttons.

HTML signatures can make business emails look more professional while giving recipients convenient ways to contact a business or visit its website.

Advantages of an HTML Email Signature

  • Creates a stronger professional image.
  • Supports business branding.
  • Can include a company logo.
  • Provides clickable contact information.
  • Can include social media links.
  • Can promote a website or service.
  • Can include a professional call-to-action.

Advantages of a Plain Text Signature

  • Simple to create.
  • Works in almost every email environment.
  • Uses very little formatting.
  • Easy to edit.

Which Is Better for Businesses?

For most businesses, consultants, freelancers, agencies, and professionals, a well-designed HTML email signature is the better choice because it combines contact information with branding and useful links.

However, a plain text signature can still be useful when simplicity and maximum compatibility are more important than visual branding.

Best Choice for Professional Communication

If your goal is to build a professional brand through every business email, an HTML email signature can provide more opportunities than a plain text signature.

A good HTML signature should be clean, responsive, easy to read, and compatible with common email clients such as Gmail and Outlook.
