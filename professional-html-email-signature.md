## What is an HTML Email Signature?

An HTML email signature is a designed email footer that contains business information such as:

- Name and job title
- Company logo
- Profile photo
- Phone number
- Email address
- Website link
- Social media icons

It can include clickable links and work across different email platforms.

## Why Businesses Need a Professional Email Signature

- Improve brand identity

- Help customers contact you quickly
- Promote your services with every email
