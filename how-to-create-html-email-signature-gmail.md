<h2>What Is an HTML Email Signature?</h2>

<p>
An HTML email signature is a professionally designed email footer that uses HTML formatting to display text, links, images, and branding.
</p>

<p>
Unlike a basic text signature, an HTML signature can include:
</p>

<ul>
    <li>Your name and job title</li>
    <li>Company logo</li>
    <li>Profile photo</li>
    <li>Phone number</li>
    <li>Email address</li>
    <li>Website link</li>
    <li>Social media links</li>
    <li>Clickable buttons</li>
    <li>Brand colors</li>
</ul>

<p>
A well-designed signature can make business emails look more professional.
</p>

<h2>Why Use an HTML Email Signature in Gmail?</h2>

</p>

A professional signature can help you:

<ul>

<h3>2. Company or Brand</h3>

<p>
Add your company or personal brand name if applicable.

<h3>3. Contact Information</h3>

<p>
Include useful contact details such as:

<ul>

<h2>How to Add an HTML Email Signature to Gmail</h2>

<p>
Gmail allows users to create email signatures through its settings.
</p>

<p>
A basic process is:
</p>

<ol>
    <li>Open Gmail.</li>
    <li>Open <strong>Settings</strong>.</li>
    <li>Select <strong>See all settings</strong>.</li>
    <li>Find the <strong>Signature</strong> section.</li>
    <li>Create a new signature.</li>
    <li>Add your formatted signature content.</li>
    <li>Choose when the signature should appear.</li>
    <li>Save your changes.</li>
</ol>

<p>
The exact Gmail interface may change over time, so always check the current Gmail settings if an option looks different.
</p>

<h2>Tips for a Professional Gmail Signature</h2>

<h3>Keep It Short</h3>

<p>
A signature should provide useful information without becoming a full advertisement.
</p>

<h3>Use Readable Fonts</h3>

<p>
Choose simple fonts and make sure the text is easy to read on both desktop and mobile devices.
</p>

<h3>Make Contact Details Clickable</h3>

<p>
Clickable phone numbers, email addresses, and website links make it easier for customers to contact you.
</p>

<h3>Use Your Brand Colors</h3>

<p>
If you have established brand colors, use them consistently without making the signature difficult to read.
</p>

<h3>Test on Mobile</h3>

<p>
Many people read emails on smartphones. Always check that your signature looks good on smaller screens.
</p>
